English

Glitch Clips is a desktop publishing tool run by the Glitch Clips team on its own computer. It uploads short gaming videos made by Glitch Clips to the Glitch Clips accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram (@glitchclipsfr and @glitchclipsgg).

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It only acts on accounts owned by Glitch Clips, after the account owner has signed in and authorized it.

Content

  • Every video is reviewed by a person before it is published.
  • Videos follow each platform's community guidelines and terms.
  • Clips use footage we captured ourselves, official media released for sharing, or short excerpts of public streams. We contact each streamer, credit the original creator in every video and caption, and remove a creator's clips within 48 hours if they ask.
  • AI-generated voice-overs are labelled as AI-generated content on each platform.

YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it with a YouTube channel, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also our Privacy Policy.

Rights holders

If you own content that appears in one of our clips and want it removed, write to us and we will take it down promptly.

Liability

The tool is provided as is, without warranty. Glitch Clips may change or stop it at any time. These terms are governed by French law.
